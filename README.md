One line, at most 120 characters: the funniest weather forecast for a city
that does not exist. Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-forecast-for-a-city-2a8769cf).

## The forecast

> Wobbleford: light drizzle of gravy, heavy chance of second breakfast, and winds that apologize for being late.

110 characters. Wobbleford is an invented city where it rains gravy, second
breakfast is a weather event, and even the wind minds its manners — a flat
official bulletin that breaks on its own weather. Nothing in the line is
wasted: every clause is either the place, the precipitation, or the wind.

## How it works

`main.ts` assembles the forecast from three bulletin parts (place,
precipitation, wind), checks it against the gate — one line, 1–120
characters, no trailing whitespace — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with Node 22.18 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
